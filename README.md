## What We Do
When companion pets go missing, panic sets in fast. We help distressed owners across London bring their pets safely home using gentle, proven methods:
- **Scent Trails & Safe Feeding Stations**: Familiar bedding and food to safely guide pets home without scaring them.
- **Night-Vision Trail Cameras**: Silent, motion-activated cameras to monitor overnight activity when pets feel safe to explore.
- **Targeted Local Search & Canvassing**: Checking sheds, outbuildings, gardens, and distributing high-visibility flyers to alert neighbors.
