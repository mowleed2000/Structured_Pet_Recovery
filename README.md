## ⚡ Mission Overview
When companion animals go missing in high-density urban environments like Greater London, standard search methods often trigger acute panic and drive pets beyond their initial displacement vectors.

The **Urban Animal Recovery Taskforce** replaces chaotic amateur searches with structured, clinical nocturnal containment protocols:
- **Phase 01: Behavioral Compression Mapping**: Modeling escape vectors, feline instinctual hunkering, and weather variables to isolate the 150-foot radial micro-habitat.
- **Phase 02: Perimeter Surveillance Grid**: Deploying night-vision, FLIR thermal telemetry, and silent motion-triggered 4K cellular recon nodes between 01:00 and 04:30.
- **Phase 03: 360-Degree Neighborhood Saturation**: Algorithmic leaflet propagation and coordinated outbuilding inspections without acoustic disruption.
